Our Mission

During Lebanon's humanitarian crisis, families struggle to access basic necessities. Sawa was created to bridge this gap—connecting those in need directly with compassionate donors and established organizations.

We believe in the power of direct connection. By removing intermediaries, we ensure that every donation reaches its destination quickly and transparently, and every person in need gets the support they deserve.

How to Donate (How Sawa Works)

Simple, transparent, and direct—connecting help with those who need it most.

For Donors

  1. 1 Recharge your balance securely on the platform
  2. 2 Browse campaigns from families and organizations
  3. 3 Make a donation directly to the campaign
  4. 4 Track impact with real-time updates

For Those in Need

  1. 1 Create a campaign with your story and needs
  2. 2 Share your information securely and transparently
  3. 3 Connect with donors and organizations directly
  4. 4 Receive support from NGOs and compassionate donors

Direct Connection, No Middlemen

Sawa eliminates third-party intermediaries. Donors connect directly with families and organizations, ensuring maximum transparency and impact. Every donation goes directly where it's needed most.

Terms and Conditions

By using the Sawa platform, you agree to comply with our community guidelines. Donors must ensure the funds they use are legally obtained. Organizations and individuals requesting help must provide accurate and truthful information.

Sawa reserves the right to suspend any account found to be engaging in fraudulent activity to protect our community of donors and recipients.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. We collect only the necessary information required to facilitate donations and verify campaigns. We will never sell your personal data to third-party marketing companies.

For recipients, your location and contact details are kept strictly confidential and are only shared with verified organizations facilitating your aid.
