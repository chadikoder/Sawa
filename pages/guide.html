The basics

Donate to a campaign

~1 min

Browse verified campaigns by category, location, or urgency. When you donate, Sawa shows the platform support fee before you confirm — no hidden cost.

  • Members pay a 5% support fee. Guests pay 10%.
  • You're redirected to your chosen provider (Whish Money or card) for the payment.
  • Your donation lands in Activity & Bills with a downloadable receipt.

Save on fees

Use your wallet

~2 min

Top up once, donate many times. The wallet gives you a lower fee and a single transaction history for your records.

  • Top up from the wallet section using any supported method.
  • Donating from the wallet saves on per-donation provider fees.
  • Every top-up and donation is listed in your transaction history.

Recipients & NGOs

Create or follow a campaign

~3 min

If you represent an organization or a family in need, you can create a campaign. Every campaign is verified before it's visible to donors.

  • Pick a clear title, a goal amount, and a category.
  • Add real photos — donors trust campaigns they can see.
  • Post regular updates so donors can follow the progress.

Trust signals

Set up your profile

~1 min

A complete profile builds trust with campaign creators and other donors. Take 1 minute to fill it in.

  • Upload a clear photo — even a simple headshot works.
  • Write 1–2 sentences in your bio about why you support Sawa.
  • Your wallet balance and history live in the profile summary cards.

Keep Sawa safe

Report suspicious activity

~30 sec

If something looks off, flag it. Use the Report button on any campaign card to send it to admin review.

  • Complaints — general issues with the platform.
  • Campaign Report — flag a specific campaign as suspicious, fake, or offensive.
  • Admin review — the admin team takes the final action.
